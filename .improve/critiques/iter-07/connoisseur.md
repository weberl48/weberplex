# Connoisseur critique, iter-07 (Dana)

I plan every group outing on Partiful and I love the moment the house lights go down. This is judged only from the iter-07 evidence folder. Timestamps are ms from the tap, as in the file names.

**Protect this:** the sealed red WEBERPLEX card in a chasing-bulb frame that turns over to the poster and then names the why ("Gma, Hal and Sam thumbed it up"), with a one-tap **Count me in** that lands on a named ADMIT ONE stub (GUEST Kim, No. 865412). That is the most Partiful-grade thing here. Don't sand it down.

## The signature moment, frame by frame

### 0. The link opens (jo-open-*, gma-open-*). Verdict: fragile at frame one, slightly slow after that
- **13 ms (Jo) / 71 ms (Gma): a pure white screen** (`jo-open-0013ms.jpg`, `gma-open-0071ms.jpg`; mean luminance 255). A white flash right before a lights-down gets it exactly backwards, and at 10 PM in a dark living room it is glaring. Nothing was filmed between 13 and 584 ms, so I can't say how long it lasts.
- **584 to 1265 ms: a red pleated curtain that hangs still**, with a valance and a warm footlight glow at the bottom (`jo-open-0948ms.jpg`). It's handsome but blank: no title card, no "Weberplex presents", no "Friday at Hal's". About 0.7 s of dead air reads as loading, not as anticipation.
- **1432 to 2530 ms: the curtain parts** from a center slit, and "The movie is…" plus the card slide into view (`jo-open-1583`, `1748`, `1914`, `2064`…). This is good theatre. The card is in view at 2597 ms median (Gma 2640).
- Gma, the newcomer, gets exactly the same opening (`gma-open-contact.png`). That's fine: the sealed card is self-explanatory.

### 1. Before the tap (jo-reveal-wait-*). Verdict: none
"The movie is…", a sealed claret card ("WEBERPLEX", a big "?", "FRIDAY 8 PM AT HAL'S"), "TAP TO TURN IT OVER". The bulbs do a real alternating chase: every other bulb is lit, and the pattern shifts between `jo-reveal-wait-0000ms` and `-0230ms`. This is the best "something is about to happen" screen in the product.

### 2. The turn (jo-reveal-turn-*, gma-, kim-). Verdict: forgettable (`taste`)
- 0 ms card back. **149 to 157 ms: edge-on. 284 ms: the poster is already showing.** By 584 ms the poster has settled. The title "Alien" fades in at 738 ms (tap to title 684 to 695 ms). Then "Friday 8 PM at Hal's" (898 to 1064), then the guest row (1228), then "thumbed it up" (1405 to 1569). The CTA arrives after 1569 ms.
- **The house never goes down at the moment of truth.** "The movie is…" and the whole surround stay at full brightness in every turn frame, 0 to 1569 ms. There's no hush, no beat and no dimming between the tap and the answer. The film starts about 280 ms after Jo taps.
- **The payoff is the same size as the setup.** The poster lands in the exact rectangle the card back filled: about 58% of the width, with the same frame in the same place (`jo-01-reveal-landed.png`). In a cinema the screen becomes the brightest and biggest thing in the room. Here the answer is a card swap.
- All the anticipation (curtain, sealed card) is spent *before* Jo does anything. Her own tap, the one moment she owns, gets a standard UI flip.
- After the landing, the bulbs blink *fully off* rather than chasing: dark in `gma-reveal-turn-0761`, `1102`, `1265` and `jo-reveal-turn-1064`, `1569`, against all-lit in `gma-0937`. In a still frame the marquee looks like it lost power just as the movie appears.

### 3. Landed (jo-01, kim-01, gma-02, run-s2). Verdict: none, apart from a generic guest row
- "Alien / Friday 8 PM at Hal's / 4 coming: you, Gma, Hal and Sam / Gma, Hal and Sam thumbed it up / **See my ticket**". Kim and Gma get **Count me in / Not this time** instead. The why-line is exactly right.
- The guest row is tiny initials (G H J S) in dark-maroon circles on near-black, with grey text beside them. It's the least personal thing on the screen. Partiful's guest list is faces, and here it's a monogram strip.
- Seed 2 renders the identical reveal (Alien, Hal's, same people; `run-s2/jo-01`, `gma-02`). So no evidence covers a long title or a different poster on this screen. Friday the 23rd's leader is "The Grand Budapest Hotel" (`jo-02-ticket.png`), so that case is next week's real session (**GUESS**: see C14).

### 4. See my ticket / Count me in, then the ticket (jo-02, kim-02, gma-04). Verdict: forgettable (`taste`)
- **46 ms** (Kim 52) from tap to ticket: the full-screen black theatre is replaced by the ordinary page, scrolled to "Up next". The stub is lovely: ADMIT ONE, notches, perforation, a vertical "No. 237982", GUEST Jo. But it fills only the top ~22% of `jo-02-ticket.png`. Directly under it come **Add to calendar · You're in · Download the invite · Watch in Stremio · 4 coming · CONCESSIONS · Popcorn/Candy/Drinks/Pizza · "I'll bring…" · Add · ALSO PLANNED · Count me in**. The payoff lands in the lobby, next to the coat check.
- Nothing about the hand-off is tactile. Gma's capture shows the stub's paper at rgb(196,177,161) against Jo's settled rgb(241,219,198) (`gma-04-ticket.png`), which suggests a plain fade-in. No print, tear, or slide out of the reveal is visible (**GUESS**: confirm by filming the See-my-ticket tap frame by frame).
- On Gma's 360 px phone the stub crumples. WHEN wraps to four lines, "Friday, / October / 16 at / 8:00 PM", and RUNNING breaks to "1979 · 117 / min" (`gma-04-ticket.png`). At 412 px it holds two lines (`jo-02`).

### Verdict against the reference
Partiful bar: the RSVP from the reveal is genuinely one tap (52 ms to ticket for Kim), and the why-line beats Partiful. The guest list is behind. Cinema bar: the curtain is a real lights-down, but it plays at page load and parts onto a second gate. The tap that finally answers "what are we watching?" gets no dim, no hush and no growth, and the ticket that should feel handed to you sits in an admin page.

## The rest of the session

**Jo: "8 updates since yesterday at 10:03 PM", opened with Walk me through (jo-03 to jo-11). Verdict: forgettable**
- Card 1 ("Here's the program. Walk through it, or call it read.") already lists all 8 updates. Steps 2 to 9 then re-read that list one card at a time ("1 OF 9" against "8 updates").
- Card 2, **"Locked for Friday: Alien / Hal's Friday night, 8:00 PM."** (`jo-04-news-2`), re-announces the signature moment seconds after Jo turned the card and got her ticket. It's a rerun of the reveal in ordinary type.
- Card 6, **"1 new thumbs-up on 1 movie / Alien"** (`jo-08-news-6`): a big thumbs icon alone in an empty dashed box, about a movie that is already locked. This is filler.
- Card 7: the name pills are tall ovals with "Gma", "Kim", "Fran", "Sam", "Hal" jammed against the top edge (`jo-09-news-7`), and it looks broken.
- Media wells on cards 2, 3, 4 and 9 are wide dashed boxes with one small thumbnail in the corner (`jo-04`, `jo-05`, `jo-06`, `jo-11`), which reads as wireframe. Card 8 has no visual at all.
- Good: "Sam is hosting Friday… [Count me in]" and Forrest Gump's inline thumbs chip are actionable, and "Fran gave Inception 4 stars / Loved it, the ending got me." has a human voice.

**Jo: page top (jo-12). Verdict: generic**
The marquee ("NOW BOOKING / ALIEN / FRIDAY, OCTOBER 16 / AT 8:00 PM · AT HAL'S / Starts in 2 days") and the ticket right under it say the same four facts, so the first screen spends both its hero slots on one message. "Starts in 2 days" is set in a Courier-style typewriter face that matches nothing else on the page and looks like a font fallback (**GUESS** whether intended).

**Jo: full page (jo-13). Verdict: none**
Calendar, shelf, Watched and Stats ("1 NIGHT WATCHED / 2 HOURS OF MOVIES / 7 MOVIES ADDED / 18 THUMBS-UPS" on a letterboard) and "You and Kim agree 50%" have real personality. It's long, but every section earns its place.

**Gma: Count me in, then the name form (gma-03). Verdict: confusing**
After she taps Count me in on the reveal (34 ms), the black theatre drops away to "Put your name on the guest list". The same idea is said twice: "Your first name is fine; this phone remembers it." / "One step left: your name, and you're on the list." Directly below, the Up next card still shows a live **Count me in** for the night she just said yes to. That's two competing calls to action on one screen.

**Gma: name, then ticket (gma-04, 849 ms). Verdict: slow (polish)**
Her first payoff takes 849 ms, against 52 ms for Kim. "Welcome, Gma. You're in for Friday." is warm.

**Gma: shelf and thumbs-up (gma-06, gma-07). Verdict: none**
Posters carry the page. The thumbs-up turns amber with the count 3 → 4 and her "G" joins the face row. That's clean.

**Gma: calendar, then "I'm free that evening" (gma-08, gma-09). Verdict: fragile**
The welcome toast sits over the "I'm free that evening" button (`gma-08-day-panel`). The follow-up toast, "You're free Sunday. If someone hosts, you're on the list.", is clear copy.

**Gma: How it works (gma-10 to gma-13). Verdict: confusing**
The SAMPLE illustrations are good, and "Then the house lights go down on everyone's phone" is the best line in the product. But step 4 promises "everyone coming gets a veto; a tie spins the reel". No veto control appears on any shelf card she used (`gma-06`/`gma-07`: thumbs, Seen it, Stremio, Plot). Through all four steps, the "You're free Sunday…" toast stays pinned over the header.

**Hal: I can host, slip, Put it on the marquee (hal-01 to hal-04). Verdict: slow and generic**
The slip ("NOW BOOKING / MOVIE NIGHT AT HAL'S / … / Put it on the marquee · Not yet") is delightful and appears in 42 ms.
- The confirmation takes **1212 ms median, 1427 worst**, the slowest tap in the session. The other single-tap actions land in 34 to 52 ms.
- The toast says "**It's on the marquee.**" Yet the night lands under ALSO PLANNED (`hal-04`), and the marquee still books Alien (`jo-12`).
- Once he's hosting, the panel is a pile of five same-weight outlined controls: "8:00 PM ▾", "Change time", "No theme ▾", "Lock the movie now" and "Cancel night" (`hal-03`). The destructive "Cancel night" is styled exactly like "Change time", and a Change time button sits next to a time dropdown.
