# Redesign: the basement theater look (2026-10-16)

The guest page takes the look of the redesign prototype (ticket stock, a
film-strip menu, poster tickets on desktop) without changing what the site does
or what it stores. The marquee at the top stays the original.

## The rule

Presentation only. Nothing that reads or writes data changes:
`firestore.rules`, `public/js/actions.js`, `store.js`, `logic.js`,
`firebase.js`, `config.js`, `api/`, `lib/` and the Home Assistant hooks are
untouched, so every record already in Firestore means what it meant before. No
new fields, no new collections, nothing new kept in the browser (only `ps.me`).

## Keep (as is, restyled)

Everything the live site does:

- Up next: Count me in, If it locked now, Tonight's ballot (one vote, one
  write-in, one veto each), host tools, the ticket, Add to calendar, snacks,
  reviews on the night.
- The calendar and its day panel (I'm free, hosting slip, ‹ ›), Subscribe.
- The shelf: Add a movie (search, Trending, New this year, For you, Surprise
  me, Paste a list), sort and show filters, thumbs-ups, Seen it, the trailer
  in the screening room, Stremio, Plot, Remove, Leading and Booked ribbons.
- Watched (rating stub, reviews, every night so far, hosts) and Stats.
- The name gate, Not you?, How it works, Since you last looked, the house
  curtain, the reel, the reveal, toasts, Android back, offline handling.
- The owner console (`/host`) keeps its current look: it still loads
  `site.css` alone.

## Change (presentation)

- Type: Bungee for marquee letters and stamps, Fraunces for headings and
  titles, Bricolage Grotesque for reading and tapping, DM Mono for numbers.
- Color: the room is Midnight (dark, warmer than before: brown-black, a red
  curtain, amber bulbs). Paper (tickets, the program, the name window, the
  ballot, the stat board) stays paper.
- The marquee stays the original (2026-10-17, the user's call): the same
  plush frame, Limelight title and board as before, in one column at every
  width. The redesign's letter-tile title, the admit stub under it and the
  next-move button were shipped on 2026-10-16 and taken back out a day later.
- The jump links become a sticky film strip that lights the section you're in.
- Section heads get outlined frame numbers; ballot rows become numbered tickets
  with a torn stub; the calendar is a printed program card; shelf cards stand
  on wooden planks; concessions is a menu board; past nights are ticket stubs.
- Desktop (980 px and up): the old marquee on top in its 46rem column
  (nothing beside it), the film strip edge to edge, the ballot as poster
  tickets, the calendar beside its day panel.

## Drop (prototype only)

- The prototype's sample data, its preview drawer and its style-sheet overlay.
- The in-page "projection booth" (house picks, season title and home switches):
  the owner console already does the first two; the house automations live in
  Home Assistant.
- Matinee (the prototype's light room) and its toggle, for now. Every live
  component was tuned for a dark room; a light one needs a contrast pass over
  all of them (paste rows, browse tiles, the hosting slip, news cards and so
  on) before it ships. Following the phone's setting would store nothing new.
- The phone's pinned bottom bar: it would sit over the controls and error
  toasts at the bottom of the screen.

## How it's built

`public/css/weberplex.css` loads after `site.css` on the guest page only: it
remaps the tokens, scopes a set of paper tokens onto the paper components (so
the same buttons and fields read in ink on cream there), and restyles the
components. The markup changes are small: frame numbers in the section heads
and the film strip. In `app.js` the only addition is the strip's scroll-spy.
The marquee keeps site.css's own rules: weberplex.css gives it back the
Limelight, Archivo and Courier Prime fonts and its original amber and board
colors.
