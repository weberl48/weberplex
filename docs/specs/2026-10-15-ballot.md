# Tonight's ballot — how a night's movie gets picked (2026-10-15)

The group chose option 2 (ferger: 2 > 4 > 3 > 1; marshman998: 2, with most
thumbs-ups getting priority on the short list; Guy: 2 or 4), plus option 4's
write-in: everyone coming can put one movie of their choice on the ballot.

Shared constraints from `2026-10-13-loop-stats-fun.md` still apply (no new
deps, nothing new in browser storage, pure logic in `logic.js` shared by the
browser and the server, writes in `actions.js`, `firestore.rules` is the

quiet in `tests/news.test.mjs`).

## What people see

- **Up next (planned night):** "What the people coming want" becomes
  **Tonight's ballot**. Each row shows the poster, title, the thumbs from the
  people coming, a small "Nobody coming has seen it" tag when true, its votes
  and who cast them.
- **Voting:** everyone coming (marked free, or the host) has **one vote**.
  - Tap **Vote** on a row to vote. Tap another row to move your vote there,
    and tap your own vote again to take it back.
  - Votes stay open until the movie locks (24 h before showtime). The host's
    Lock now closes them early.
- **Write-ins:** each person coming may put **one movie of their choice** from
  the shelf on the ballot ("Add your pick": a picker over the movies the night
  can pick, searchable by title). It shows "Sam's pick". They can swap it or
  take it back.
- **Vetoes and themes still work.** Vetoed or off-theme movies never appear on
  the ballot, and a write-in that gets vetoed drops off.
- **Locked night:** the card shows how it was decided: "Jaws won the vote,
  3–1" / "Tied 2–2, the reel picked Jaws" / "No votes, so the most-thumbed
  pick won".
- **How it works** (the first-visit walkthrough): its thumbs/pick card is
  rewritten to explain the ballot in two sentences.

## How the ballot is drafted — `ballotFor(night, { movies, votes, seen, attendees, watched })`

1. Start from what the night can pick: `pickable()` (watched and other
   nights' locked movies out, vetoes out, theme in — with the existing
   fallbacks).
2. Rank by **thumbs from the people coming** (desc). Thumbs come first, per
   the group. Ties break by: nobody coming has seen it first, then fewer
   people coming have seen it, then thumbs from everyone, then title.
3. Take the **top 5**.
4. Add every **write-in** from someone still coming (if pickable) and every
   movie that currently holds a **vote** from someone still coming, even if it
   has dropped out of the top 5. No duplicates.
5. Return rows `{ movie, thumbsComing, seenComing, votes: [nameKeys], writeInBy }`
   in ballot order (top-5 order, then write-ins / kept-by-vote).

## The pick at lock — `pickMovie` keeps its signature, adds `ballotVotes`, `writeIns`, `seen`

- Count votes from people **still coming** on movies **on the ballot**.
- **Most votes wins**; a tie goes to the reel among the tied (`tie` lists them).
- **No votes at all:** the top of the ballot wins (most thumbs from the
  people coming, then the tie-breaks above). If even that's a tie, the reel
  picks among those tied on thumbs. If nobody coming has thumbed anything,
  the old fallbacks apply unchanged (most thumbs from anyone, then anything).
- `basis` becomes `'ballot'` when votes decided it; otherwise the existing
  `'coming' | 'everyone' | 'shelf'`. The lock records `lockedVotes`
  (`{ imdbId: count }` for the ballot at lock time) so the card can say how it
  was decided.
- All callers keep passing the same inputs plus the new ones: the browser
  clock and Lock now in `app.js`, `lib/tick.js`, and the leader in
  `lib/status.js`. Server reads add `seen` only when a night is due (tick) or
  planned (status).

## Data (on the night doc, so the clock needs no extra reads)

- `ballotVotes`: map `nameKey → imdbId` (one vote each), with `ballotVoteAt`
  `nameKey → server time` (for the news).
- `writeIns`: map `nameKey → imdbId` (one each), with `writeInAt`.
- `lockedVotes`: written by the lock (see above). It is cleared by unlock and
  rewritten by the next lock.
- Vote and write-in writes use field paths (`ballotVotes.<key>`, `deleteField()`
  to clear), like vetoes, so concurrent voters never collide.
- **Dropping out** (`setFree(…, false)` on a planned night) clears that
  person's veto, vote and write-in in the same batch. Unlock keeps votes and
  write-ins.

## Rules (`firestore.rules`)

- Generalise `vetoOk` into one helper for "one person's entry in a per-person
  map": exactly one key changes, in the map and its stamp map together. That
  key belongs to the host or to someone free that evening. A set value is a
  movie on the shelf with its stamp `== request.time`, and a cleared entry
  clears its stamp.
  - Vetoes keep "set or clear only" (no in-place change).
  - Votes and write-ins may change in place (moving your vote is one write).
- New planned→planned transitions: `changed.hasOnly(['ballotVotes',
  'ballotVoteAt'])` and `changed.hasOnly(['writeIns', 'writeInAt'])`.
- `newNight` allows these fields only as empty maps or absent.
- Lock may also write `lockedVotes` (a map, at most 40 entries), and unlock
  may null it.
- Old night docs without any of these fields keep working (`.get()`
  everywhere).

## Tests

- **logic:**
  - `ballotFor` ranking: thumbs first, unseen tie-break, top 5, write-ins and
    voted movies kept, vetoed/off-theme excluded, people who dropped out
    ignored.
  - `pickMovie`: the vote winner, a tie goes to the reel, no votes falls back
    to the ballot top, no thumbs uses the old fallbacks.
- **rules:**
  - Vote, move a vote, and take it back.
  - A write-in, and swapping it.
  - Not allowed when you're not coming; one key per write; concurrent voters
    both kept.
  - Dropping out clears all three; old docs still lock and unlock.
- **status / tick:** a locked night honours the votes, and the leader follows
  the ballot.
- **news:** the guard passes with the two new kinds, and fixtures exist for both.
- **e2e:**
  - Two guests and a host vote, a guest adds a write-in, and a vote moves.
  - A lock honours the vote and the locked card shows the tally.
  - Without votes the most-thumbed wins.
  - Dropping out removes your vote.
